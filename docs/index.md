Kioku is a context infrastructure platform that captures, stores, and retrieves knowledge from meetings, documents, and conversations.

If you're here, you likely want one of these:

- **Self-host Kioku** — full control over your data and deployment
- **Use the CLI** — interact with your knowledge base from the terminal
- **Connect via MCP** — let AI clients search your meeting context
- **Build on the API** — integrate meeting transcripts and knowledge search into your apps
